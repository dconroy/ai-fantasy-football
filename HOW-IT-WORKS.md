# How this thing works (no nerd stuff)

This is our draft-night weapon. It watches the draft, does the math, and tells
you who to pick — you still make the actual pick in the Yahoo app. Nothing here
touches your Yahoo team.

---

## Getting in (2 minutes, once)

1. Open the site. It asks for the **magic word**. You know the movie. Type it.
2. Click **Sign in with Yahoo** and log in with your normal Yahoo account.
3. First time only: you'll land on a "waiting for approval" page. The admin
   (the Cobra Kai guy) taps approve, and you're in. After that you go straight
   to the board.

## First thing to do when you're in

Set **your draft slot** — the dropdown at the top left of the board. If you're
picking 8th in the draft, choose 8. Everything the app recommends is based on
where you pick, so this matters. Type your team name in the Strategy panel
while you're at it.

## The board

Everyone sees the **same draft board** — same picks, same players, same order.
When anyone records a pick, it shows up for all of us within a few seconds.

What's **yours alone**:

- **Pins (☆)** — players you love. They get a boost in your recommendations.
- **Avoid** — players you never want. They disappear from your Top five.
- **Strategy sliders** — how much the engine cares about tiers, position needs,
  and so on. Don't touch them if you don't care; the defaults are good.

Your pins and avoids are private. I can't see that you're avoiding your own
kicker from last year.

## The Top five

The panel on the left is the whole point. After every single pick it
recalculates the five best players for **your** roster, based on Boris Chen's
tier rankings plus what your team actually needs. Each one has a plain-English
reason. When in doubt, take the #1.

## "YOU'RE ON THE CLOCK"

When the draft reaches your slot, the top bar turns bright green and pulses,
and the browser tab flashes 🚨. You can't miss it. Pick a player (the green
confirm button, or the ✓ next to any player in the list) and the draft rolls
on.

## League-mates strip

Right under the top bar you can see everyone in the league, their slot, and a
**green dot** if they're on the board right now. "2h ago" means they left two
hours ago. That's how you know who's around.

## Starting a draft (admin only)

The admin presses **"Start a draft…"** and picks one of three options:

| Option | What happens | When to use it |
|---|---|---|
| **Practice mock** | Robots draft the empty seats on a timer and **pause at every real manager's slot** until that person confirms. | A full group dress rehearsal before draft night |
| **Manual mock** | Nothing moves until you press buttons. | Slowly exploring strategy solo |
| **Draft night — live** | The board follows our **real Yahoo draft** automatically. | The actual draft |

If you're not the admin you don't start anything — just show up, set your
slot, and enjoy the recommendations.

### Group practice mocks

The practice mock is a real rehearsal for everyone at once. Before starting it,
make sure each manager has **set their own draft slot** (the dropdown, or the
admin can set it in League admin) — and that everyone has a **different** slot.
When the mock reaches your slot, your screen lights up "on the clock" and the
whole room waits for you to confirm, exactly like draft night. Robots fill any
slot nobody claimed.

## Draft night, step by step

1. Everyone opens the site and the Yahoo app.
2. The admin hits **Start a draft… → Draft night — live**.
3. Draft normally **in the Yahoo app**. The board sees every pick within
   seconds and keeps your Top five fresh.
4. When you're on the clock in Yahoo, look at the Top five here, then make the
   pick in Yahoo. That's it.

## Weekly HQ (during the season)

The **Weekly HQ** link at the top shows your actual Yahoo roster with:

- The **best possible lineup** for the week, with exact "bench X, start Y" swaps
- **Injury and bye-week alarms** so you never start someone on a bye again
- **Waiver targets** ranked by Chen, and what everyone else in the league is doing

Same rule as always: it advises, you click the buttons in Yahoo.

> Heads up: Weekly HQ and live draft sync need Yahoo's Fantasy API approval,
> which is pending. Everything else works now.

## FAQ

**Does this auto-draft for me?** No. It never touches Yahoo. You make every
pick yourself in the Yahoo app.

**Why is my name "Yahoo ABC123"?** Yahoo didn't tell us your real name. Ask
the admin to rename you, or ignore it — your team name shows most places.

**I see "on the clock" but it's not my turn in Yahoo.** Check your draft slot
dropdown — it's probably still on the default.

**Can I mess up the shared board?** Members can record picks and manage their
own pins/avoids. Resets, mocks, and sync are admin-only, so no.
